--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SETUP
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Install the APK on an Android phone.
2. If the paywall / “Try free” screen appears, tap the Portion Track logo 7 times to unlock. Do not subscribe.
3. Install Health Connect (and Google Fit or Samsung Health) if needed.
4. Open Settings → Steps → turn Connected ON → allow Steps in Health Connect.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TEST STEPS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Exercise → Today
   - Confirm the Steps card shows today’s steps and goal progress.

2. Exercise → Yesterday (and 1–2 older days that have steps in Health Connect)
   - Confirm the Steps card updates for that day.
   - Confirm the count matches Health Connect for the same date.

3. Settings → Steps
   - Confirm “Today’s steps” shows.
   - Confirm “Recent steps” lists about the last 14 days.
   - Spot-check 2–3 dates against Health Connect.

4. Settings → Steps
   - Confirm there is no privacy line like “Steps stay on your device” or “We do not upload this data.”

5. Settings → Steps
   - Turn Connected OFF → go to Exercise → Today → confirm connect CTA (no live count).
   - Turn Connected ON again → confirm today’s steps and recent history return.

6. Weight tab
   - Log/update a decimal weight (e.g. 150.5).
   - Confirm Starting / Current / Goal values stay on one line (no wrap like “150.” / “5”).
