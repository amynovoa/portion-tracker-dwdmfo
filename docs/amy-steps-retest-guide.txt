================================================================================
SETUP STEPS (do this first)
================================================================================

1. Open Portion Track (after unlock if needed).
2. Go to Settings → Steps.
3. Turn Connected ON.
4. Allow Steps permission in Health Connect when prompted.
5. Confirm “Today’s steps” shows a number (or 0 if you have not walked yet).
6. Go to the Exercise tab and confirm a Steps card appears for Today.

Pass if connect works and today’s steps show.
Fail if connect errors, crashes, or says Health Connect is missing from this build.

================================================================================
TEST 1 — Steps on Exercise for TODAY
================================================================================

Goal: Today’s steps show on Exercise after connect.

Steps
1. Open Exercise tab.
2. Make sure the day picker is on Today.
3. Look for the Steps card under the day picker.

Pass if
- You see today’s step count and progress toward the goal
- Number roughly matches Health Connect / Fit for today

Fail if
- No steps card when connected
- App crashes
- Count is clearly wrong vs Health Connect for today

================================================================================

Goal: Past days show steps from Health Connect (main request).

Steps
